THE PROGRAM

WHAT'S INCLUDED & WHO WE TRAIN

Elite pitching development built on data, science, and proven programming. Every component is designed to maximize velocity, durability, and game-day performance.

Ideal For

  • High school athletes chasing next-level opportunities

  • College pitchers building velocity and pitch shape

  • Pro pitchers returning from injury or building durability

  • Athletes who want clear data and accountability

Requirements

  • Baseline Metric Testing Evaluation

  • Commitment to throwing plans, strength work, and arm care

  • Coachability and willingness to apply feedback

  • Consistent training frequency aligned with your goals and season demands

Trackman Integration

Every bullpen is tracked with Trackman for velocity, movement, and pitch design.

Phase-Based Programming

Your throwing plan adjusts for off-season, in-season, and recovery phases.

Arm Care & Recovery

Post-throw arm care and mobility work to support durability.

Strength Training

DSI-directed training to build power, force output, and resilience.

1-on-1 Bullpens

Included in the base program at every location for individualized feedback.

BSF App Access

Your plan, schedule, and progress are all organized in the BSF App.
