A comprehensive, year-round hitting development program built on the science and the art of hitting.

GET STARTED BOOK A CALL

Complete hitters are built physically and mentally.

BSF Hitting is a year-round development program that combines advanced technology, individualized instruction, strength training, and game-like hitting environments. Led by Bobby Atkinson, Director of Hitting Performance, every hitter starts with a full assessment, trains twice a week in the Hitting Lab, and builds power in the weight room, with progress tracked the whole way.

OUR APPROACH

THE SCIENCE & THE ART

Measurable power and a game-ready approach, developed together.

The Science of Hitting

We measure what drives damage at the plate, then train it. Swing metrics, batted-ball data, and athletic testing show where each hitter's power comes from.

Bat Speed Exit Velocity Rotational Power

The Art of Hitting

Power only matters if it shows up in the box. We coach the swing and the hitter: mechanics, approach, and how they prepare and compete.
