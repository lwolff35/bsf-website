Complete hitters are built, physically and mentally.

BSF Hitting is a structured, year-round training program designed to develop complete hitters, both physically and mentally. Every hitter starts with metric testing, follows an individualized development plan, trains in the Hitting Lab, and builds strength, speed, and athleticism in the weight room, all tracked in one performance profile.

THE PROGRAM

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Six pieces, one system. Every component is built around the individual hitter and measured from day one.

13+
Who it's for

Hitters ages 13 and older who are committed to improving their offensive performance.

01

Metric Testing Baseline

Every hitter begins with a comprehensive testing session to establish baseline metrics, including bat speed, exit velocity, rotational power, and other key indicators. This data identifies strengths, areas for improvement, and serves as the foundation for each athlete's plan.

02

Individualized Hitting Development Plan

A personalized, easy-to-follow plan that includes preparation, drills, and training environments designed around each hitter's individual needs.

03

Sports Performance Training

A customized strength, speed, and athletic development program based on individual assessments and designed by Brett Velon, Director of Sports Performance.

04

BSF Performance Profile

A personalized performance dashboard that tracks key hitting metrics, testing results, and progress throughout the program.

05

Small-Group Hitting Lab Sessions

  • Two guaranteed, bookable sessions per week
  • 45-minute sessions
  • Maximum of four hitters per group
  • Structured instruction in the Hitting Lab
06

Cage Membership Access

Additional cage access for independent training and skill development.

  • Monday–Friday: 3–5 PM and 8–10 PM
  • Saturday: All day
