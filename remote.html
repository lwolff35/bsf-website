Elite coaching with structure, accountability, and results.

BSF Remote gives you a dedicated 1-on-1 performance coach, clear daily training, and direct communication through the BSF App, with decisions built around your recovery, throwing volume, and season. Using our remote assessment and video feedback, we deliver training decisions specific to you, not guesswork, to support your long-term career trajectory even when you are not training in person.

HOW REMOTE TRAINING WORKS

01

Assessment and Coaching Setup

We start with your training history, current movement profile, injury background, and throwing schedule to set clear priorities. Then you are assigned one dedicated BSF performance coach, so your communication, mentorship, and adjustments stay consistent from week to week.

02

Build Your Program

Your coach delivers a remote pitching program inside the BSF App that aligns with your current season phase and positional demands. Every training block includes throwing progressions, recovery targets, and strength work that evolves with your response and schedule.

03

Submit Video, Get Feedback

Upload bullpen clips, drill work, and lift footage directly in app for coach review. You receive video analysis pitching mechanics feedback with specific corrections, cues, and drill progressions so changes transfer quickly to game speed.

04

Progress Through Phases

Your training does not stay static: we re-evaluate execution quality, readiness, and performance markers each phase. That process keeps workloads aligned to your calendar and ensures your development keeps moving forward, not just busy.
