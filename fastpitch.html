THE PROGRAM

WHAT'S INCLUDED & WHO WE TRAIN

B.S.F. Softball Pitching & Overhand Throwing Performance is semi-private training for athletes with high throwing demands, built to create college-level habits and long-term consistency.

Ideal For

  • Ages 12+ pitchers focused on velocity and command

  • Position players with high overhand throwing demands

  • Travel and high school athletes preparing for recruiting

  • College athletes seeking advanced development and durability

Requirements

  • Metric Testing Evaluation optional and available upon request

  • Flexible training model with up to 6 sessions per week

  • Coachability and consistency with training assignments

  • Ages 12+ (programming scaled to age and training background)

Video & Ball Flight Data

Video and ball-flight data guide adjustments to pitch quality and command.

Overhand Throwing Development

Arm path efficiency and position-specific mechanics improve power and accuracy.

Workload Management

Periodized throwing plans keep arms healthy while maintaining development.

High-Performance Routines

Build game-day readiness through preparation routines and mental skills work.

College Recruiting Guidance

For athletes pursuing the next level, we provide structure and recruiting direction.

BSF App Access

Programming is delivered and tracked through the BSF Performance App.
