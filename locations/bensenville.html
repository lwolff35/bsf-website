MEMBERSHIP TERMS

All memberships are auto-renewing unless written notice is received before the renewal date. No refunds or credits will be issued for unused months. Memberships cannot be paused. Early cancellation fees apply to 12-month commitments. Pitching memberships require a Metric Testing Evaluation before starting training (one-time fee of $300). Fastpitch athletes can add a Metric Testing Evaluation at any time if they want one.
